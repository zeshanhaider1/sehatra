💬 Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Are these templates aligned with Punjab Healthcare Commission (PHC) MSDS?

These templates are drafted to align with commonly referenced indicators in PHC Category-II/III MSDS and similar provincial standards. They are administrative tools to help organize documentation; inspection outcomes depend on your facility's actual implementation, records, and the regulator's review.

2. In what format are the files provided, and can we edit them?

All documents are provided in fully editable Microsoft Word (.docx) and Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) formats. Each document features clear placeholders like [Clinic Name] and [Doctor Name], allowing you to insert your clinic details, doctor registration numbers, and letterhead in minutes.

3. How quickly do we receive the files after payment?

Our delivery target is within 60 minutes after payment confirmation. Once your transfer screenshot is shared on WhatsApp, we send the Google Drive link and a compressed ZIP file. If there is any delay, we will update you on WhatsApp.

4. Can clinics and hospitals in Sindh (SHCC), KPK, or Islamabad (IHRA) use these toolkits?

Many operational areas (infection control, biomedical waste segregation, staff credentialing, and informed consent) are similar across provincial regulators such as SHCC, KP-HCC, and IHRA. However, requirements can vary by province, category, and facility type, so you should review and adapt the templates to your local regulator's current checklist.

5. Do you provide guidance if an inspector requests specific compliance registers?

Yes. Every kit includes a quick-start organization checklist, and our support desk is available on WhatsApp to guide you on how to fill, sign, and organize your files. We provide documentation support, not regulatory approval guarantees.

6. Kya Sehatra healthcare consultancy bhi provide karta hai?

Ji haan. Sehatra clinics aur hospitals ke liye healthcare consultancy/documentation support provide karta hai: Online Document Preview & Review (Rs. 5,000) aur On-Visit Consultation & Audit Guidance (Rs. 15,000). Hum gaps identify karne, files arrange karne aur practical next steps batane mein madad karte hain. Ye administrative/documentation support hai; regulatory approval guarantee nahi.
