MedStock — Pharmacy & Store Stock Control

MedStock turns the pharmacy register into a live stock system: what is on the shelf, what is about to expire, what each supplier delivered and where every item went. It installs on one Windows computer and prints the stock records an inspection asks for.

Stock records that survive an inspection

  • Batch and expiry tracking: see what is expiring before it reaches the counter.
  • Supplier records: who supplied what, at which rate, and when.
  • Issue records: movement from store to pharmacy counter, OPD or ward.
  • Stock value dashboard: quantity, value and movement at a glance.
  • Printable reports: stock registers and movement sheets ready for a visiting team.
  • Single PC, no server: installs in minutes on a normal Windows computer.

The pharmacy findings auditors repeat

Pharmacy and store reviews most often fail on the same three points: expired items on the shelf, purchase and issue records that do not reconcile, and no temperature or stock movement record. MedStock addresses the first two directly by making batch, expiry and movement visible before the inspection rather than during it.

For ward-level dispensing and hospital stores, the same register covers multiple locations, so the store keeper and the pharmacy counter work from one set of numbers.

Works with the rest of your paperwork

MedStock reports can be printed and filed alongside Sehatra's infection control, CQI and HR documents, so a single audit file holds the complete picture. Where a hospital also runs LabMan, stock and supplier records sit in two systems that speak the same language.

Software is supplied for administrative and operational record keeping only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment guidance, and pricing is quoted per facility by users, branches and modules.
