LabMan — Laboratory Management System

LabMan is a complete laboratory information management system for diagnostic laboratories in Pakistan. It handles patient registration, barcoded samples, result entry and verification, branded PDF reports, quality control, stock, billing and a full audit trail — on a single Windows computer, without an internet connection.

Free PHC/MSDS consultation included

Every LabMan installation includes a free PHC/MSDS documentation consultation — register formats, record retention and the evidence a laboratory inspection asks for. There is no separate consultancy fee.

Everything a working laboratory needs in one system

  • Patients and registration: MRN-based registration, referring doctor, contact history and repeat visits.
  • Samples that cannot be mixed up: barcoded collection, receiving, rejection reasons and a full chain of custody.
  • Results with accountability: technician entry, pathologist verification, critical-value alerts and a locked record after release.
  • Reports patients trust: branded PDF reports with your laboratory logo, watermark, reference ranges and delivery tracking.
  • Quality control: Levy-Jennings charts, analyser records and calibration history kept alongside the results they support.
  • Money side handled: invoices, partial payments, receipts, expenses, daily cash summary and profit and loss.
  • Stock that matches the register: inventory, purchase orders, supplier records and stock adjustments.

Registers that inspections ask for

Most laboratory findings during a PHC/MSDS review come from missing or inconsistent records rather than missing equipment. LabMan keeps those records inside one system so they can be produced on demand: patient registers, sample logs, result audit trail, quality control sheets, calibration records, stock movement and staff activity.

Records are filterable by date, department and user, and can be printed or exported when a visiting team asks for them. Access is role-based, so reception, technicians, doctors and the pathologist each see only the screens their role allows.

Deployment that fits a Pakistani laboratory

  • Runs on a normal desktop: Windows 10 or 11, 4 GB RAM and about 1 GB of disk space. No server and no database installation.
  • Works without internet: the database is a single file on your own computer, so a dropped connection does not stop reporting.
  • Network ready: one installation can serve several counters on the clinic network.
  • Your data, your backup: one-click export and import, plus scheduled copies of the database file.
  • Setup and training included: installation, test catalog and rate list import, report layout and staff training are part of onboarding.

Already running in live laboratories
