Clinic OS — Clinic & OPD Management

Clinic OS runs the front desk of a clinic: reception queue, appointments, patient registration, invoicing, staff attendance and doctor panels. It is deliberately simple — a receptionist can learn it in a morning — and it keeps working when the internet does not.

Free PHC/MSDS consultation included

Every Clinic OS installation includes a free PHC/MSDS documentation consultation — patient and OPD registers, attendance and staff records, and the formats an inspection expects to see. There is no separate consultancy fee.

One screen for the whole OPD

  • Reception: today's appointments, one-click check-in and live patient status from scheduled through to discharged.
  • Appointments: booking with auto-generated numbers, doctor selection and daily schedule view.
  • Patients: registration with unique IDs, contact details and visit history.
  • Billing: invoices with discounts, automatic totals and a daily income summary.
  • Staff: attendance, duty and salary records without a separate register.
  • Doctors: panels, specialisations and OPD load.

Built for staff who are not computer people

Clinic staff are hired to look after patients, not to learn software. Clinic OS keeps the screens short, uses plain language, works in English and Urdu, and runs offline in the browser so a slow connection never stops the queue.

Data stays on the clinic computer. One-click export gives you a dated backup file, and the same file restores the clinic on a new machine if the old one fails.

From daily cash to inspection day

Every clinic keeps a patient register, an OPD log, an attendance sheet and a daily cash summary. When these are written in a notebook, staff spend the evening rebuilding them. Clinic OS produces them as the day happens, so the same records can be printed for the owner, the accountant or a visiting inspection team.

Attendance, duty and salary records also feed the staff file requirements that PHC/MSDS reviews examine. Pair Clinic OS with Sehatra's HR dossier pack and both sides match.

Software is supplied for administrative and operational record keeping only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment guidance, and pricing is quoted per facility by users, branches and modules.
